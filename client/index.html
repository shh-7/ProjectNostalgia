📖 How To Play

1
Guess the Secret Word

Letters appear as blanks. Guess letters on screen or type directly on your keyboard.

You have 6 lives. Each wrong guess draws a part of the hangman figure (head, body, arms, legs).

3
Turn-Based Multiplayer

Take turns with your opponent. Correct guesses award +10 points and maintain momentum. Guessing the full word gives a +50 round victory bonus!
